Planning your website

Choosing a pay monthly website package

Look beyond the headline price and get clear on the services, support and terms that matter to your business.

By TradeBuilt · 25 September 2026

Pay monthly websites spread the cost of your online presence. To compare packages properly, start with what your business needs, then look closely at what each service includes.

Define the job your website needs to do

Write down your core services, the areas you cover and the action you want visitors to take. A small business with one main service may need a different structure from a team offering several specialist services.

Be clear about whether you need a new logo, help with writing or support choosing photographs. These tasks should be discussed as part of the scope.

Ask what happens after launch

Check what website care and support mean in practice. Ask about content changes, hosting, security maintenance and how to request help.

If search optimisation is included, find out whether this means initial setup or ongoing work. Ask for the agreed activities and review process to be explained clearly.

Understand the commercial details

  • Is VAT included in the displayed price?
  • Are there setup fees or separately charged services?
  • Who owns the domain, content and website?
  • What happens to hosting and access if you cancel?
  • If you pay annually, how are cancellation and refunds handled?

Get the answers in writing before you commit. Clear expectations make it easier for both you and your provider to work well together.

Choose the right fit

The useful comparison is the overall service, not just the lowest monthly figure. Look for an approach you understand and a package that reflects your business priorities.
