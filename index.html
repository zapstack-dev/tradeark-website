GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity.
A lot of confidence.

Choosing a website should feel straightforward. Here’s what you need to know.

All your questions, answered
Am I tied into a fixed contract?

No. TradeBuilt offers no fixed contracts and you can cancel anytime. We will confirm how cancellation affects your billing, hosting and website before you sign up. Annual payment and refund arrangements will also be confirmed before purchase.

What does the monthly price include?

The draft packages show the proposed services and shared inclusions. Final prices, page allowances and support details will be agreed before you commit. All prices are shown excluding VAT.

Can you help if I already have a website?

Yes. Tell us what is working, what is frustrating you and what you want to improve. We can discuss a new website, search optimisation or a more consistent visual identity.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. The website will be designed around the way customers use their phones, with clear text, easy navigation and prominent enquiry options.

Is SEO included with every website?

Website setup and ongoing SEO are different services. The proposed packages include technical foundations, while ongoing content and search work depend on your agreed package. We will make that distinction clear before you sign up.
